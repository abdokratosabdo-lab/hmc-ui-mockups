A neighbourhood clinic that grew into nine specialties.

Hajj Medical Center has served Naccache and the surrounding Metn area since 2001. What began as Dr. Marcel Hajj's private practice now houses medical and dental clinics side by side on the ground floor of Green Zone A, Building 71.

How we work

Patients are seen by the specialist they need without being sent across town. Allergy, nutrition, cardiology, gynecology, pediatrics, physiotherapy, psychology, esthetics and dentistry share one reception, one file and one phone number.

The center also runs a WhatsApp secretary line and an AI assistant for routine questions, so appointments and results do not depend on office hours.
